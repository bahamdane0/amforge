CSV Cleaner

Drop in a messy CSV file and get back trimmed, normalized rows with empties removed.

Drag & drop a file, or click to browse

Files stay on your device. Max 5MB on the free plan.

What this tool does

Trims stray whitespace from every cell, strips a leading byte-order mark, normalizes line endings, and drops fully empty rows.

How it works

DataConverter Forge parses your CSV with a quoted-field-aware parser, trims each field, removes rows where every cell is empty, and re-serializes clean CSV with consistent line endings.

Why use DataConverter Forge for this

  • Fixes stray spaces left by spreadsheet exports
  • Removes blank rows that break downstream imports
  • Keeps quoted commas and line breaks intact

Frequently asked questions

Does this change my column values, not just whitespace?

No — only leading/trailing whitespace is trimmed from each cell; the values themselves are preserved.

What counts as an empty row?

A row where every single cell is empty (after trimming) is removed.
