XLSX to CSV Converter

Drop in an Excel workbook and get back a plain CSV file of its first sheet.

Drag & drop an Excel file, or click to browse

Files stay on your device. Max 5MB on the free plan.

What this tool does

Converts the first sheet of an Excel workbook into a plain CSV file, entirely in your browser.

How it works

DataConverter Forge reads the workbook with the SheetJS library and serializes the first sheet to CSV, preserving quoting for cells containing commas or line breaks.

Why use DataConverter Forge for this

  • No upload — the workbook is parsed locally
  • Handles quoted values correctly
  • Works with both .xlsx and legacy .xls files

Frequently asked questions

Which sheet gets converted?

The first sheet in the workbook. Use Excel Sheet Extractor (coming soon) to target a specific sheet.

Are number formats and formulas preserved?

Formulas are converted to their last calculated value; cell formatting is not preserved in plain CSV.
