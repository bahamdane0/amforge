What this tool does

Turns CSV rows into a formatted Excel workbook (.xlsx), preserving your header row and every column.

How it works

DataConverter Forge parses your CSV with a quoted-field-aware parser, builds a worksheet from the resulting rows with SheetJS, and packages it as a downloadable .xlsx file.

Why use DataConverter Forge for this

  • Correctly handles quoted commas and embedded line breaks
  • Produces a real .xlsx workbook, ready to open in Excel
  • Nothing leaves your browser tab

Frequently asked questions

Does the first row become the header?

Yes, the first row of your CSV is written as the first row of the worksheet, typically your column headers.

Can I convert a very large CSV?

Files up to the free plan's size limit work, but only the first 100 rows are shown in the preview — the full sheet is included in the download.
